About the project

The project turns legal expertise from a one-to-one support model into a digital platform with AI. It automates typical HR scenarios — dismissals, bonuses, remote work — lowers the cost of a first consultation and raises engagement through content and recommendations.

Functionally it is an AI bot bounded by the Russian Labour Code and BLS cases, with document generation, user file uploads and a materials base: videos, articles, templates. A human expert joins on request.

Goals
  • From a P2P model to a digital solution — less manual work, scalable expertise
  • Automating and lowering the cost of typical HR consultations
  • Accessibility: a low entry price through AI and a subscription model
  • Higher engagement through the knowledge base, recommendations and access to an operator
  • A B2B product: workspaces, access rights, analytics, customization
  • A knowledge base and monetization of the company’s expertise
