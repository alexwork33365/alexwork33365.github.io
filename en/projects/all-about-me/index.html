All About Me

A cross-platform iOS and Android app for self-discovery and daily emotional support through astrology, numerology and Human Design: a personal natal chart, card of the day, daily astro climate, in-depth personality report, personal astro calendar, compatibility and report history.

About the project

The app builds personalised recommendations and visual charts from precise user data: name, gender, date, time and place of birth. One interface brings together the natal chart, the numerology profile and the basic Human Design parameters with interpretations for every element.

User scenarios are described in 152 use cases across 12 areas: sign-up and profile, card of the day, astro climate, push notifications, in-depth report, astro calendar, compatibility, payments and subscription, history, settings, export and sharing, assistant.

Goals
  • Natal chart, numerology and Human Design in one personalised interface
  • Daily engagement through the card of the day, astro climate and notifications
  • Hybrid monetisation: a free tier, one-off products and a subscription
  • An architecture ready for feature growth after the MVP

Key functionality
  • Sign-up via email, phone or social networks; a profile that automatically builds the natal chart, numbers and HD data
  • Interactive circular natal chart with animation and hints on planets, numbers and design centres
  • Card of the day: a daily metaphorical card with an illustration and an inspiring reading
  • Daily astro climate: planetary transits and aspects in plain language
  • Push notifications: card of the day, notable astro events, personal recommendations for subscribers
  • In-depth personality report — a one-off purchase or a subscription
  • Personal astro calendar by subscription: a daily forecast from the natal chart and current transits
  • Compatibility with another person in love, business and emotions — a one-off purchase
  • Report history synced by account and available offline
