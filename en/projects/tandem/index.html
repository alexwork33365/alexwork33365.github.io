About the project

A two-sided marketplace with a high average ticket: tack, feed and equipment, services of vets, trainers and farriers, horse sales. Deals between private individuals are protected by holding the funds until confirmation.

User scenarios are described in 83 use cases across five areas: account, goods, services, chats, horses — down to horse comparison, reviews and complaints about a listing.

Goals
  • One venue to list and find goods, services and horses
  • Safe deals between private individuals
  • Trust through profiles, reviews and ratings
