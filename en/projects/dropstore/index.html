DropStore

A non-monetized internal merch store for VK employees with an in-house currency, “Drops”: catalogue, purchases, personal account, delivery to the office or home, a CRM module for orders, products and warehouses, VK Teams integration and analytics.

About the project

DropStore is not about revenue: it drives engagement and company culture. Employees receive Drops every quarter and spend them on branded goods and merch.

Behind the storefront sits a full CRM: collections (“drops”), stock control, an order status model with nine statuses, delivery handling by the facilities team, user and balance management.

Goals
  • Higher employee engagement
  • Stronger company culture
  • Motivation through an in-house currency and limited merch drops
