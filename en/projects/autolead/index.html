AutoLead

Digitising the Excel-based lead and payment tracking of a car-dealer call centre: a web interface with a backend that automatically receives leads and chats from the call-tracking CRM, processes them and shows them in an admin panel with filtering, logging and export.

In production · stage 1
About the project

Before the project all data was processed by hand in Excel, even though leads and chats came from a call-tracking CRM. Stage one replaced the spreadsheets with an admin panel: webhook intake, duplicate detection, a client directory, Excel export, an audit log and manual payment entry for ROI.

Administrator scenarios are described in 40 use cases across 11 panel sections: sign-in, clients, analytics, spam, inactive, leads, chats, history, integrations, dictionaries and accounts.

Goals
  • Replace manual Excel tracking with automatic intake from the CRM
  • Give the call centre one panel with filters, export and an action history
  • Calculate ROI per client from leads and payments
  • Handle up to 10,000 leads a day with concurrent users
